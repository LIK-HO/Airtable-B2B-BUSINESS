# Airtable Schema

## Business layer
1. 01 Клиенты — canonical B2B entity.
2. 02 Контакты — people and contact points.
3. 03 Потребности — qualified opportunities/needs.
4. 04 Заказы — operational order lifecycle.
5. 05 Задачи — operator work queue.

## Technical layer
6. 90 Аудит — audit ledger.
7. 91 Безопасность — security/data-safety registry.
8. 92 Конфигурация — controlled parameters and version markers.
9. 93 Миграции — migration/recovery ledger.

Technical tables are outside the operator surface.
